Villa Park Little League Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

The Villa Park Little League app provides schedules, standings, scores, tournament information, field maps, and league resources.

Information Collection

The app does not require users to create accounts and does not sell personal information. Information submitted through league score reporting forms is used solely for league operations and game management.

Data Usage

Information is used only to provide app features, league communications, schedules, standings, and tournament information. Personal information is not sold to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The app may use standard third-party services necessary for app functionality, hosting, analytics, or notifications.
